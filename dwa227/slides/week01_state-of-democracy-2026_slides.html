The vocabulary · V-Dem’s “Regimes of the World”

Four regime types, one ladder

Liberal democracy — free and fair elections plus rule of law: courts and legislatures that constrain the executive, and protected individual rights. (Where 7% of humanity lives.)

Electoral democracy — multiparty elections that are genuinely free and fair, with real freedoms of expression and association — but weaker checks on those who win. (Where V-Dem now places the USA.)

Electoral autocracy — elections are held, but the playing field is rigged: media, opposition, and courts tilted toward incumbents. The world’s most common regime type. (Russia, Türkiye — and India?) Is India an electoral autocracy? ▶

Closed autocracy — no multiparty elections for those who actually rule; opposition politics is shut out altogether. (China, Saudi Arabia, North Korea.)

The democracy/autocracy line: are multiparty elections free and fair? The liberal add-on: is the winner constrained?

Lührmann, Tannenberg & Lindberg, “Regimes of the World” (2018); V-Dem, Democracy Report 2026, pp. 10–11
