Section 04
Key Takeaways for Our Class

You're pragmatic about AI's future

Nearly two-thirds of you agree AI skills will matter for your careers (avg 3.93/5) — even those who have reservations about using it now.

Academic anxiety is real

62% of you scored 4 or 5 on worry about being accused of dishonesty — even for legitimate uses. This class has a clear policy: disclosed, critical AI use is encouraged.

You read, don't draft

69% use AI to explain concepts. Only 12% have used it to generate assignment drafts. You're already drawing the line most educators would want you to draw.

ChatGPT dominates — Claude doesn't

90% use ChatGPT Free. Only 2 students have used Claude. That changes today. Different tools have different strengths, biases, and blind spots.

Environmental concern is a theme

Multiple students raised AI's environmental costs unprompted. This is a legitimate IPE issue — data centers, energy use, and displacement are all on the course syllabus.

Most of you are open but novice

52% are open to AI roleplay but new to it. Only 12% have done it before. The Jolt is designed exactly for this: low-stakes, critical, guided engagement.
