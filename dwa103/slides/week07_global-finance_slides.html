Part I · Bhagwati 1998

Growth without capital account convertibility

  • China and Japan: “remarkable growth rates without capital account convertibility”
  • Western Europe: “return to prosperity was also achieved without capital account convertibility”
  • Gains from free capital mobility: “asserted, not demonstrated”1–3
  • “most of the payoff can be obtained by direct equity investment”
Research since 1998 · financial openness and growth

1 Kose, Prasad, Rogoff & Wei 2006: “it remains difficult to find robust evidence that financial integration systematically increases growth, once other determinants of growth are controlled for” (NBER Working Paper 12484: 15; published in IMF Staff Papers, 2009)

2 Rodrik & Subramanian 2009: “The stylized fact that there is no correlation between long-run economic growth and financial globalization …” (IMF Staff Papers 56(1): 112)

3 Ostry, Loungani & Furceri 2016, IMF Research Department: “Although growth benefits are uncertain, costs in terms of increased economic volatility and crisis frequency seem more evident.” (Finance & Development 53(2): 39)

Part IV · Oatley et al. 2013

Flat and hierarchical networks

  • “In a flat network, a crisis anywhere in the system can spread everywhere.” (p. 133)
  • Hierarchical: “‘robust-but-fragile’: the system is robust to peripheral disturbances but fragile in the face of disturbances in the center.” (p. 136)
