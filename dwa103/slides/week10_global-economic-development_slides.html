Part II · The state and development · Olson 1993
A secure autocrat has an encompassing interest in his domain that leads him to provide a peaceful order and other public goods that increase productivity. Whenever an autocrat expects a brief tenure, it pays him to confiscate those assets whose tax yield over his tenure is less than their total value.
…autocracies will rarely have good economic performance for more than a generation. The conditions necessary for a lasting democracy are the same necessary for the security of property and contract rights that generates economic growth. Olson 1993: 567 (abstract)

      Development · Banerjee & Duflo 2011
      Economists (and other experts) seem to have very little useful to say about why some countries grow and others do not. Basket cases, such as Bangladesh or Cambodia, turn into small miracles. Poster children, such as Côte d’Ivoire, fall into the “bottom billion.” In retrospect, it is always possible to construct a rationale for what happened in each place.       World Bank strategy, 1944–2026

      1944
      Bretton Woods creates the IMF and the World Bank. IBRD Articles, Art. I(i): To assist in the reconstruction and development of territories of members.
      1948
      After first loans to France and other European countries, Chile receives the first non-European loan ($13.5 million, hydroelectric power).
      1960
      International Development Association (IDA) established: finance for poorer countries on softer terms than IBRD loans.
      1973
      President McNamara (1968–81) uses the term absolute poverty in his Annual Meeting speech.
      1980
      First structural adjustment loan: $200 million to Turkey (March 25).
      1996
      President Wolfensohn, Annual Meetings: we need to deal with the cancer of corruption.
      1999
      Poverty Reduction Strategy Papers endorsed: nationally-owned, participatory poverty reduction strategies become the basis for concessional loans and debt relief.
      2013
      Twin goals: end extreme poverty (to no more than 3% by 2030) and promote shared prosperity (incomes of the bottom 40%).
      2023
      Evolution Roadmap: vision of a world free of poverty on a livable planet.
      2026
      Industrial Policy for Development published; Chief Economist Gill, The Right Way to Do Industrial Policy (March 17).
