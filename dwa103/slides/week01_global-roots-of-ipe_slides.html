Helleiner 2023: Two Claims

1 · A global history

Pre-1945 debates included thinkers across Asia, Africa, Latin America, the Middle East.

Idea flows ran in multiple directions — East→West, South→North.

2 · Beyond three orthodoxies

Debates exceeded the liberal–neomercantilist–Marxist triad.

Also present: autarkism, environmentalism, feminism, pan-movements, regionalism.

Feminism · Environmentalism, pre-1945

  • Feminist political economists: global wealth relied on unpaid and undervalued women's labor
  • Environmental critics: industrial capitalism linked to resource depletion and long-term impoverishment
  • Both literatures predate the field's 1970s formalization
Helleiner 2023, chs. 7–812

Pan-Movements

  • Pan-Africanism: Du Bois, Garvey — economic solidarity across the African diaspora
  • Pan-Islamism, Pan-Asianism: transnational religious or civilizational identity as economic basis
  • Unit of solidarity: not the nation-state

Course Terms, DWA 103

Public good

Non-excludable, non-rivalrous — clean air, national security.

Free-rider problem

Benefiting from public goods without paying — NATO spending, climate agreements.

Principal–agent problem

Agent acts on own interest under asymmetric information.

Path dependence

Choices at time T constrained by T−1 — QWERTY, USD hegemony.
