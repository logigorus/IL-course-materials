Shearer Professorship ✦ NEW
›
Recently announced
New endowed Ambassador/Diplomat in Residence honoring Derek Shearer (Chevalier Prof, McKinnon Center director, 20+ yrs). Former U.S. Ambassador to Finland under Clinton.
What it means: DWA will always have Ambassador/senior diplomat in residence. Students learn from practitioner at highest levels. Doors to internships, mentorship, foreign service.
Network effect: Shearer linked students to Washington, UN, embassies worldwide. Professorship institutionalizes that. Direct line to foreign service — through a working relationship, not a career center.
For parents: Very few undergrad programs have permanent Ambassador in Residence. Endowed = guaranteed. Students graduate with network most don't build until grad school.

LA as classroom — Port + Mayor
›
Quotable moment
"Next Friday, I'm taking my students to the Port of Long Beach. We're meeting with ILWU Local 13 — the dock workers' union — and seeing firsthand how global supply chains, labor politics, and environmental policy collide at the busiest port complex in the Western Hemisphere."
What students see: Container ships from Asia. Automated cranes. Air quality monitors. Tariffs on the manifest. GPE on the ground.
Labor: Local 13 — automation, trade disruptions, energy transition. People who literally move the global economy.
Ecology: EJ ground zero in SoCal. Diesel, ship emissions, zero-emissions transition.
Mayor's Office: Young Initiative + LA Mayor's Office → localizing UN SDGs. Global frameworks → city-level policy on climate, housing, inequality.
For parents: Your child stands where 40% of US container imports arrive, talking to dock workers, environmentalists, policymakers.
